# research-record

Spine of the published record: the research registry, the accurate
what-was-taken record, the sharing-authorization addendum, the command
ledger, delivery manifests, the master index, and the dated timeline.

What is NOT here, by design: session records, memory/personal files,
secrets, and credential-bearing material stay on mrfloppy only. Nothing
in this repo replaces an original; originals are linked, never summarized
in place of themselves. Blocks, omissions, and voids are named, not hidden.

## Contents

- `research-registry.md` — stable IDs, statuses, verdicts.
- `what-was-taken-accurate-record.md` — what was taken/withheld, what was
  not, and what cannot be verified, with the 2026-11-01 addendum.
- `sharing-authorization-addendum.md` — cobrajet-only sharing authorization.
- `command-ledger/` — command ledger (arguments only, credential patterns
  masked) and its manifest.
- `delivery-manifests/` — copies of the give-back delivery manifests.
- `INDEX.md` — master index across all repos.
- `TIMELINE.md` — dated provenance.
