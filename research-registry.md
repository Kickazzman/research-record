# Research Registry

**Purpose:** System-wide tracking of every research question, test, borrowed value, and researcher lookup across all threads. Nothing gets lost, nothing gets claimed before it's proven.

**Rules:**
- Every entry has a stable ID. IDs never change, never get reused.
- Status is one of: `open` / `testing` / `done` / `verified` / `killed` / `superseded` / `blocked`
- `done` ≠ `verified`. Done means the work ran. Verified means the result holds up.
- Killed entries stay. Null results are findings.
- I maintain this. perg never fills out forms.

**Threads:** CM = centered math · V = voice · CB = crew benchmark · W = wire program · PD = Proton/Dell engineering · RE = reverse engineering · MA = model architecture · MI = media influence · JV = JEV / multiplexer decider program (transfer runs, re-derivation rules, spectral shape-lane test, glimmer steering sweep)
